Security requirements before deployment

Recommended minimum controls for any real scanning service.

◎
Domain ownership

Prove control using DNS TXT or file-token validation.

⌁
SSRF protection

Resolve DNS safely and block private, loopback, link-local and metadata addresses on every redirect.

⌚
Scan limits

Enforce strict timeouts, redirect caps, response-size limits and rate controls.

♙
Secrets & access

Keep credentials server-side; use least privilege, audit logs and authenticated access.
